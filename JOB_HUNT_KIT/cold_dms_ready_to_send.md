# Cold Outreach — Ready to Send (5 Messages)

> **Time to send**: 30 minutes
> **Strategy**: 5 personalized DMs to Tier 1 founders + 2 follow-ups

---

## ⚠️ Important Personalization Steps

Before sending, do these 5 things:
1. **Find the founder's name** (LinkedIn search, company About page, Twitter)
2. **Read their latest blog post / tweet / launch announcement** (last 30 days)
3. **Reference ONE specific thing they did** in the personalization column
4. **Adjust the tone** if the founder is more formal/casual
5. **Send between 9 AM - 11 AM** (Tuesday-Thursday best)
