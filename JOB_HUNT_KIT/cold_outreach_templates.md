# Cold Outreach Templates — 5 Ready-to-Personalize

---

## Template 1: Cold DM to a Founder/CEO at an AI Startup

**[OPENING LINE]**
> Hi [First Name] — saw [specific thing: their launch / YC announcement / blog post / open-source repo]. The [specific feature] caught my eye because [one-sentence reason why it's interesting].

**[BODY]**
> I'm Venu — I build local-first AI systems. My latest is JARVIS: an automation platform that runs 25 workflows (job scraping, LinkedIn outreach, content generation) entirely on local Ollama models — no cloud AI APIs, $0 marginal cost, runs on a laptop. The reason I'm reaching out: [Company]'s work on [their specific domain] is the kind of problem where local-first matters — privacy, latency, cost. I'd love 15 minutes to learn what you're building and see if there's a fit for a founding-team engineer who'd rather ship than write essays about shipping.
>
> Quick demo if curious: [JARVIS Loom link]
>
> — Venu

**[WHEN TO USE]** Founders of seed-to-Series-A AI startups in India. Skip if the founder has 5K+ employees or the company is too early (just an idea, no users).

**[PERSONALIZE BEFORE SENDING]**
- [ ] Replace [specific thing] with a real recent post, launch, or repo from them (search LinkedIn / Twitter / blog — last 30 days)
- [ ] Replace [specific feature] with their actual product feature name
- [ ] Replace [their specific domain] — read their About page once
- [ ] Confirm JARVIS Loom link is your latest 5-min demo

## Template 2: LinkedIn Message to a Hiring Manager

**[OPENING LINE]**
> Hi [First Name] — saw your team's [specific thing: open-source repo / conference talk / engineering blog post]. The [specific technical decision] reminded me of how I solved [similar problem] in my own work.

**[BODY]**
> Quick context: I'm Venu, I build AI automation systems. My current project (JARVIS) runs 25 workflows locally with Ollama + Docker + Playwright + Grafana — no cloud APIs, designed to fit in 8GB of RAM. I used [their-relevant technique] to handle [their-relevant problem], and I think there's overlap with what your team is doing.
>
> If useful, here's a 5-min Loom showing JARVIS end-to-end: [link]
>
> Are you hiring Full Stack / AI engineers this quarter? Happy to send a one-pager or jump on a 20-min screen share.

**[WHEN TO USE]** Engineering managers or tech leads who publicly ship technical work. Avoid pure HR recruiters for this one — they don't appreciate the technical depth.

**[PERSONALIZE BEFORE SENDING]**
- [ ] Reference a SPECIFIC repo, talk, or post from them — link it
- [ ] Mention the actual technical decision they made (e.g., "your choice to use Rust over Go for the parser")
- [ ] Replace [their-relevant technique] with something they actually use (Kafka, Postgres, RAG, etc.)
- [ ] If you can, mention a mutual connection or shared community

## Template 3: Reply to a Recruiter's Outreach Message

**[BODY]**
> Hi [Recruiter Name] — thanks for reaching out about the [Role Title] role at [Company].
>
> Quick context that might help: I'm Venu, an MCA AI/ML student based in Hyderabad. I've built JARVIS, a local-first AI automation platform (25 workflows, Docker + Ollama + Playwright + full observability stack) — it's open source on my GitHub. The reason I think the [Role Title] role is a fit: I've actually shipped [specific capability from their JD — e.g., "a parallel scraping system with semaphore-based concurrency control"], not just studied it.
>
> A few things I'd want to understand:
> - Team size and how the [their team/product area] is structured
> - Whether the role involves building from zero or scaling an existing product
> - Expected comp range for 0–1 years experience
>
> Happy to do a quick intro call this week or next. Resume and Loom demo attached.
>
> — Venu
>
> GitHub: github.com/venu0807
> JARVIS Loom: [link]

**[WHEN TO USE]** Recruiter messages you on LinkedIn or email about a specific role. Don't use this template for unsolicited outreach — it's a reply, not an opener.

**[PERSONALIZE BEFORE SENDING]**
- [ ] Pull 1–2 specific requirements from their JD and reference them
- [ ] Adjust the "few things I'd want to understand" list based on the role
- [ ] Attach resume + Loom link
- [ ] If they're an external recruiter (not in-house), clarify the role and company before sending

**[BODY]**

>
> I'm Venu Gopal Reddy, an MCA AI/ML student. I built JARVIS (open source on GitHub) — a local-first AI automation platform that runs 25 production workflows on a single 8GB-RAM laptop: job scraping across 4 portals, LinkedIn outreach, content generation, all wired with Prometheus + Grafana + AlertManager. Zero cloud AI cost. Zero data exfiltration.
>
> I'm looking for an entry-level Full Stack / AI Engineer role where infrastructure matters. Attached: resume. Links below for context:
> - GitHub: https://github.com/venu0807
> - JARVIS repo: https://github.com/venu0807/Jarvis
> - 5-min Loom demo: [link]
>
> If [Company] is hiring for this kind of role, I'd appreciate 15 minutes with your hiring manager. If not, no worries — happy to be redirected.
>
> Best,
> Venu Gopal Reddy
> pvgreddy3@gmail.com
> +91 [your number]
> Hyderabad (open to remote)

**[WHEN TO USE]** Companies with public careers pages where the in-house HR email is findable. Best for 50–500 employee companies. Avoid FAANG-style employers — they have separate fresher hiring pipelines.

**[PERSONALIZE BEFORE SENDING]**
- [ ] Find the actual HR person's name (LinkedIn / company team page) — never send to generic "careers@" addresses
- [ ] Reference a SPECIFIC recent company milestone (funding, product launch, hiring spree)
- [ ] Update [Feature] and [source] to match that specific company
- [ ] Attach a PDF resume — name it `Venu_Gopal_Reddy_Resume.pdf`

## Template 5: Follow-up After No Response (Day 5–7)

**[BODY]**
> Hi [Name] — following up on my message from [Day]. No pressure if the timing's off.
>
> Reason I'm bumping this: I just shipped [one specific new thing — a feature, a blog post, a deployment]. Thought of [Company] when I built it because [one-line connection to their work].
>
> If a 10-minute call doesn't make sense, totally fine — even a one-line "not now, try again in [timeframe]" helps me prioritize.
>
> Thanks,
> Venu

**[WHEN TO USE]** 5–7 days after sending Template 1, 2, or 4 with no response. Send ONCE. If still no reply after another 7 days, archive them and move on.

**[PERSONALIZE BEFORE SENDING]**
- [ ] Reference the exact date of your first message ("from Tuesday")
- [ ] Update [one specific new thing] to something you actually shipped in the last week
- [ ] Tie it to THEIR specific problem (not just "I shipped X")
- [ ] Don't apologize for following up — you're being respectful of their time, not pushy

## 🎯 Outreach Cadence (Daily Routine)

| Time block | Action |
|---|---|
| **9:00–9:30** | Send 3 cold DMs (Templates 1 + 2) |
| **9:30–10:00** | Reply to recruiter messages (Template 3) |
| **10:00–10:30** | Send 2 cold emails (Template 4) |
| **Follow-ups Friday** | Send all pending Template 5 follow-ups |

**Daily target: 5 new outreach + 2 follow-ups = ~35/week.**

**Don't:** send more than 8 outreach messages in a day — quality drops.
**Don't:** use the same template twice in a row — personalize each.
