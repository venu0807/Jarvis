## ❌ AVOID (Common Traps)

- **Google, Microsoft, Amazon, Meta** — specific campus hiring pipelines, not lateral entry. Apply only via their campus programs.
- **IBM, TCS, Infosys, Wipro, HCL** — service companies, your portfolio doesn't differentiate you
- **Crypto exchanges (CoinDCX, WazirX)** — regulatory uncertainty, hard to recommend
- **Companies with >5K employees in India but no AI focus** — your edge won't show

## 🎯 Personalization Strategy

When applying to each company, research ONE specific thing in the last 30 days:
- A product launch
- A blog post
- An open-source release
- A conference talk
- A funding announcement

Reference it in your outreach. Generic applications get ignored; specific applications get replies.
